# Expense Mannagement System
## Problem
A growing company reimburses employee expenses — travel, meals, software, training — but today it runs on email threads and spreadsheets. There's no audit trail, policy limits are applied inconsistently, and approvals are slow and easy to lose track of.

They want a lightweight internal platform — ExpenseFlow — where employees submit expense reports with line items and receipts, managers approve or reject them, finance reimburses approved reports, and everyone can track status. Company policy rules are enforced automatically at submission so bad claims never reach a manager.

This is a small, honest slice of an internal approval-workflow tool. You are building the real flow end to end and mocking only the parts that touch the outside world (file uploads, actual payments).

## Solution
 Build an applilcation that automates the approval and reimbursement system

 ## Users
 Employee , Manager, Admin

 ## Techstack 
 SpringBoot ,React ,Postgresql
